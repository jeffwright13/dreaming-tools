# Dreaming Difficulty Brief

## Purpose

Show Dreaming Spanish's existing relative difficulty information consistently wherever users encounter videos or series.

## Users

Dreaming Spanish learners who use difficulty to choose appropriate videos, especially from Library history and series views.

## Constraints

- Do not collect credentials, cookies, tokens, or account data.
- Use the public Spanish video catalog as the source of difficulty scores.
- Preserve Dreaming's native difficulty badges where they already exist.
- Support dynamically rendered cards and modals.
- Avoid dependencies on generated CSS-module class names.
